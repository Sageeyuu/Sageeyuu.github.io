I am a Statistician on the UKRI-funded project *A Multi-Dimensional Understanding of the Digital Far Right*, based in the Department of Linguistics and English Language at Lancaster University. In this role, I develop, test and evaluate statistical methods for analysing zero-inflated and sparse multivariate data.

I hold a PhD in Economics from Lancaster University. My research focuses on international macroeconomics, technology diffusion and productivity, grounded in applied econometrics and time-series methods. My work has been presented to the UK Department for Business and Trade and the National Institute of Economic and Social Research (NIESR).

I have eight years of experience teaching economics, across undergraduate courses and high-school research programmes, and I am an Associate Fellow of Advance HE (AFHEA).

## Research interests

- International macroeconomics
- Technology diffusion, innovation policy and productivity
- Applied econometrics

R, MATLAB, Stata, Python, EViews and RATS
